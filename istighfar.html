سَيِّدُ الْاسْتِغْفَارِ

Sayyidul Istighfar — the master of seeking forgiveness

Sahih al-Bukhari 6306 · the Prophet ﷺ taught it as the best form of istighfar

Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“Whoever says it during the day with firm faith in it, and dies on the same day before the evening, he will be from the people of Paradise; and if somebody recites it at night with firm faith in it, and dies before the morning, he will be from the people of Paradise.”

The Prophet ﷺ called it the master of seeking forgiveness (sayyid al-istighfar).

Sahih Sahih al-Bukhari 6306 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is the most complete form of istighfar: it opens with tawhid, admits servitude, then owns both Allah's favour and your own sin.
  • The narration attaches a promise to dying that day or night in that state — which is why it is a morning and evening practice, not a ritual.
  • It is short enough to learn properly and deep enough to keep uncovering for years.

How to recite it

  • Say it once in the morning and once in the evening — the narration names those two times.
  • Say it with firm belief in what the words mean, not as a habit with an absent heart.
  • Learn it phrase by phrase here first, with the meaning, so the confession lands when you recite it alone.
