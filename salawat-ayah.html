Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“Among the most excellent of your days is Friday… so invoke more blessings on me that day, for your blessings will be submitted to me.”

The verse establishes the command; the hadith gives Friday as the day to increase it. Both are needed to understand why Friday salawat is not an optional extra.

Qur'an + Sahih Qur'an 33:56 · Sunan Abi Dawud 1047 — Sahih (al-Albani) Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • The verse says Allah and His angels send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ — then commands the believers to do it too.
  • The Friday hadith tells you when to increase it and that it reaches him.
  • It answers the question many beginners ask: why do we say salawat at all?

How to recite it

  • Recite the ayah, then act on it — the verse is a command, not information.
  • On Friday, increase the salawat; the salawat itself is in the Salawat Ibrahimiyya lesson.
  • Learn the ayah with the recitation so the command is in your own voice, not just read.
