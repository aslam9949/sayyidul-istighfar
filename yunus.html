Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“No Muslim man supplicates with it for anything, ever, except that Allah responds to him.”

This wording is a verse of the Qur'an, so the audio is a real recitation. The Tirmidhi report is what attaches the promise of a response; sunnah.com does not print an al-Albani grade on that page, so no grade badge is claimed here beyond the collection.

Qur'an · Tirmidhi Qur'an 21:87 · Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3505 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is a Qur'anic dua, so you are supplicating with Allah's own words — the strongest thing you can say.
  • It admits fault without despair: "I have been of the wrongdoers" — which is the actual adab of asking.
  • It was said in a situation with no way out, which is why people reach for it in distress.

How to recite it

  • Say it in distress, and say it slowly — the meaning is confession, not a formula for a quick fix.
  • Learn it with tajweed from the recitation; it is one verse and short enough to master properly.
  • Do not treat it as a guaranteed outcome in a specific time; the narration is about Allah answering the supplication.
