Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“And say: My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small.”

The dua is the closing part of the verse, which is why the whole ayah is taught here — the recitation covers the full verse.

Qur'an Qur'an 17:24 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is the dua Allah Himself commanded children to make for their parents.
  • It names the reason: they raised you when you were small and could do nothing.
  • It is the dua that remains useful after a parent has passed away.

How to recite it

  • Say it for both parents, together, and separately if you wish.
  • The verse before it forbids even saying "uff" to them — the dua does not replace that conduct.
  • Learn the whole ayah with the recitation so the dua is attached to its context.
