Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“Our Lord, give us in this world good and in the Hereafter good and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.”

This is Qur'an, so the audio is a real recitation, never synthesised.

Qur'an Qur'an 2:201 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It asks for good in both lives in one line, without preferring one over the other.
  • It is short enough to say after every prayer, which is how it becomes part of your day.
  • It is the safest thing to send to a friend who asks you to make dua for them — it covers everything.

How to recite it

  • Say it after each prayer, and again when someone asks you to pray for them.
  • Note the order: good in this world first, then the Hereafter, then protection from the Fire.
  • Learn it with the recitation here; the words are few but the stops matter.
