Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“If somebody recited the last two Verses of Surat Al-Baqara at night, that will be sufficient for him.”

The Prophet's ﷺ statement is about reciting them at night; the reward is stated plainly and needs no embellishment.

Qur'an + Sahih Qur'an 2:285–286 · Sahih al-Bukhari 5009 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is the shortest nightly practice with the largest stated reward: two verses, and the narration says they are sufficient.
  • They close the longest surah with the believer's confession of faith in the angels, the books and the messengers.
  • The second verse carries "Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear" — the line people need most on hard days.

How to recite it

  • Recite both verses before sleeping; that is the occasion named in the hadith.
  • Read the meaning once line by line before you try to memorise, so the words attach to something.
  • The second verse is long — learn it in its three natural stops, not in one run.
