Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.”

This is Qur'an, so the audio is a real recitation and never synthesised.

Qur'an Qur'an 3:173 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is what was said when a frightening report arrived — the Qur'an records it as the response of faith.
  • It shifts the weight of a problem off your shoulders without pretending the problem is small.
  • It is four words. It is the one line people use most in a sudden crisis.

How to recite it

  • Say it when news frightens you, before you react.
  • Say it for a friend in trouble — it is a dua for someone else as much as for yourself.
  • Learn it with the recitation; the emphasis on "wa ni'mal-wakeel" is part of its weight.
