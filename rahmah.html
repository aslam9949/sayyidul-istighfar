Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“The supplication of the one in distress: Allahumma rahmataka arju, fa la takilni ila nafsi tarfata 'ayn, wa aslih li sha'ni kullah, la ilaha illa ant.”

Placed by the scholars of adhkar in the chapter on distress and grief (Hisn al-Muslim 123).

Hasan in chain (al-Albani) Sunan Abi Dawud 5090 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is literally the dua of the distressed — the wording the Prophet ﷺ named for that state.
  • It asks for one thing twice over: that you are never left to yourself, not even for the blink of an eye.
  • It ends where every dua should end: la ilaha illa ant.

How to recite it

  • Recite it when you feel overwhelmed, and in the morning and evening as part of your adhkar.
  • Slow down on tarfata 'ayn — it is the phrase that carries the meaning.
  • Pair it with an action: name the one matter you want set right before you say wa aslih li sha'ni kullah.
