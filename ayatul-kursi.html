Reference, virtue & method

Reference

“Whoever recites it when he goes to bed, a protector from Allah remains over him and Shaytan does not come near him until morning.”

This is Qur'an, so the audio is a real recitation and never synthesised — a voice model cannot carry the tajweed of an ayah.

Qur'an + Sahih Qur'an 2:255 · Sahih al-Bukhari 2311 · Sahih Muslim 810 Open source ↗

Why we read it

  • It is the greatest ayah of the Qur'an by the Prophet's ﷺ own statement in Sahih Muslim 810.
  • The narration ties reciting it at night to protection until morning — a daily shield for almost no time.
  • It is dense tawhid: His life, His knowledge, His throne, and the fact that nothing encompasses Him.

How to recite it

  • Recite it before sleeping, and after each obligatory prayer.
  • Learn it with tajweed from the recitation here, not from transliteration alone.
  • Take it one line at a time; the ayah is long and the meaning is heavier than the length suggests.
