Ayatul Kursi — The Verse of the Throne

Al-Baqarah 2:255 · Sahih Muslim 810

0 / 9 memorized
Al-Baqarah 2:255 · Recitation: Mishary Rashid Alafasy · Uthmani text: Quran.com · Translation: Sahih International
بَارَكَ اللَّهُ فِيكَ
Masha'Allah! You have memorized Ayatul Kursi — the greatest ayah of the Qur'an. The Prophet ﷺ asked Ubayy ibn Ka'b which ayah in the Book of Allah he considered greatest; he answered Ayatul Kursi, and the Prophet ﷺ struck his chest and said: may the knowledge delight you, O Abu al-Mundhir.
